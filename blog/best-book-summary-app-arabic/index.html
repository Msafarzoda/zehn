Best Book Summary App for Arabic Speakers in 2025

Arabic is spoken by 400 million people. Yet until recently, almost every major book summary app was English-only. Here's what's changed — and which app is actually worth your time.

The problem with most book summary apps

Apps like Blinkist and Headway have millions of users. They have great content, polished design, and a huge library. But if you want a summary of Atomic Habits in Arabic, or The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People in Russian, you're mostly out of luck.

Blinkist offers a handful of "Blinks" in German and Spanish. Arabic? Zero. Russian? A tiny selection. For the 400 million Arabic speakers and 258 million Russian speakers in the world, the leading apps have historically offered almost nothing.

If you're an Arabic speaker looking for book summaries, Zehn is the only meaningful option right now. The library is large, the audio quality is good, and the app is free to start. The other apps simply don't have Arabic content.

For English-only readers, Blinkist is still excellent. But if you want to learn in Arabic — or share content with family members who prefer Arabic — Zehn is the clear choice.
