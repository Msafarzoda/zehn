Whether you want to understand investing, build a business, or just stop being confused by money — these 10 books are where to start. Each one is available as a free 15-minute audio summary on Zehn.

The book that introduced millions to the difference between assets and liabilities. Kiyosaki's core idea: the rich make money work for them, while others work for money.
