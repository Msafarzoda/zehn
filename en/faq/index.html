General
Zehn is an AI-powered microlearning audio app that turns the world's best non-fiction books into bite-sized 15-minute audio lessons. You get the core ideas, actionable principles, and key insights — without the filler. Available on Android and iOS in English, Arabic, Russian, and Tajik.
Unlike Blinkist, Zehn is audio-first and includes an Ask AI feature so you can have a real conversation with the book. Unlike Audible, Zehn compresses full books to 15 minutes — you get the wisdom without committing to hours of narration. Zehn also supports Arabic, Russian, and Tajik, which most competitors don't.
Zehn has 5,000+ lessons across 8 categories: Leadership (820+), Productivity (740+), Mindset (680+), Business (590+), Money & Finance (520+), Relationships (490+), Health & Wellbeing (460+), and Science & Philosophy (380+). New lessons are added regularly.
Zehn is currently available in four languages: English, Arabic, Russian, and Tajik. The full app interface and all 5,000+ lessons are localized in each language. More languages are coming soon.
Zehn is available on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). It works on any smartphone or tablet running Android 6.0+ or iOS 14+.
Features
The average lesson is 15 minutes. Some run as short as 12 minutes and others up to 20, depending on the book. You can also adjust playback speed to go faster or slower — ideal for commutes or focused study sessions.
While listening to any lesson, tap Ask AI to open a chat window. You can ask anything about the book — a concept you didn't catch, a deeper dive on a chapter, or how an idea applies to your life. The AI answers using the author's actual framework and ideas, grounded in the book content rather than the open web. Free users get 3 questions per day; Pro and Lifetime users have unlimited access.
Yes. Pro and Lifetime subscribers can download lessons for offline listening — perfect for flights, commutes, or areas with weak signal. Free users require an internet connection to stream.
Yes. Every lesson has synced text that scrolls in real time as the audio plays. You can toggle it on or off from the player screen. It's great for reinforcing what you hear or catching details you might miss.
Set a daily learning goal (even just one lesson counts). Each day you meet it, your streak grows. Streaks are displayed on your Goals screen along with your recently completed lessons and monthly progress. It's designed to make consistent learning feel rewarding — not like homework.
When you first open the app, you set your learning goals, interests, and daily time commitment. Zehn's AI uses this — along with your listening history — to surface the most relevant lessons for you. You can also take the 5-question quiz on our website to get personalized recommendations before downloading.
