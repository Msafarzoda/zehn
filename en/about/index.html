Why Zehn?

Traditional learning has a problem: it demands too much time. Books take weeks to read. Audiobooks take hours to listen to. Courses demand week-long commitments.

But what if you could learn a complete book in the time it takes to commute to work? That's the Zehn promise.

Our AI-powered platform distills the core insights, actionable principles, and transformative ideas from the world's best non-fiction books into focused, 15-minute audio books. You get the wisdom without the filler.

Our Story

Zehn was founded in 2025 with a simple observation: busy professionals wanted to grow but didn't have time for traditional learning methods.

The team recognized that successful people listen to content during commutes, workouts, and downtime. Instead of forcing them to choose between learning and living, we asked: What if we compressed the essential insights from great books into the time they already have available?

Today, we're based in Dubai and serving learners across the Middle East and beyond, helping thousands of ambitious people grow smarter, faster.
