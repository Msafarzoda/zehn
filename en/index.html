Frequently asked questions

What is Zehn?

Zehn is a book summary app for iPhone and Android. It turns 10,000+ nonfiction books into 15-minute audio and text summaries, and every book has Ask AI, a chat that answers questions about that book.

How long is each book summary?

Most summaries take about 15 minutes to listen to or read. Each book page shows its exact length.

Which languages does Zehn support?

English, Arabic, Russian, Tajik, Hindi and Urdu. You can switch language inside the app at any time.

What is Ask AI?

Ask AI is a chat built into every book. You can ask it to explain an idea, give examples or help you apply the book to your own situation, and it answers from that book.

Is Zehn free?

Zehn is free to download from the App Store and Google Play. Current plans and prices are shown inside the app.

Which devices does Zehn work on?

Zehn works on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets.

How is Zehn different from Blinkist or Headway?

Zehn is built for readers beyond English, with full libraries in Arabic, Russian, Tajik, Hindi and Urdu, and it adds an AI chat to every book.
