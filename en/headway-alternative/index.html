Frequently asked questions

Is Zehn a good alternative to Headway?

Yes, if you read in Arabic, Russian, Tajik, Hindi or Urdu, or you want an AI chat on every book. Zehn has 10,000+ book summaries of about 15 minutes each, as audio with synced text. If you read in the languages Headway supports and like its features, Headway is a good choice too.

Does Headway have summaries in Arabic, Russian, Hindi or Urdu?

As of November 2026, Headway lists these summary languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Polish, Japanese, Czech, Romanian, Hungarian. Arabic, Russian, Tajik, Hindi and Urdu are not on that list. Zehn has libraries in all five, plus English.

Does Headway have an AI chat for each book?

As of November 2026, Headway's public feature list does not include a chat about each book. Zehn has Ask AI on every book: you ask a question and the answer comes from that book's summary.

Is Zehn free?

Zehn is free to download from the App Store and Google Play. Current plans and prices are shown inside the app.

Can I use Zehn and Headway together?

Yes. Many readers keep one app for English and use Zehn for books in their own language and for Ask AI.
